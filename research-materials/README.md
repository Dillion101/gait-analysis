# Research materials

- `extraction-sheet.xlsx` — the working literature-review workbook, containing the article inventory, strength and weakness coding, technique frequencies, screening parameters, and research objectives.
- `final-report.docx` — the submitted unpublished project documentation.
- `presentation.pptx` — the final project presentation.

The third-party papers reviewed during the project are not redistributed in this public package. Their citations are preserved in the report and extraction sheet.

The extraction workbook records ScienceDirect, IEEE Xplore, and Springer Link in its numeric search funnel. The narrative report mentions Scopus in its methodology. This repository preserves the original materials rather than silently changing that discrepancy.
