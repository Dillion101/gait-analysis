# Research materials

The University of Ghana does not permit public distribution of final-year project
files, so the project documentation, presentation, and literature-review workbook
are not included in this repository.

The following are available on request by email to `damartey008@gmail.com`:

- The submitted project documentation. Unpublished and not peer-reviewed.
- The final project presentation.
- The literature-review extraction workbook, containing the article inventory,
  strength and weakness coding, technique frequencies, screening parameters, and
  research objectives.

The documentation and presentation are co-authored with Jake Sompa Asamoah and
were supervised by Dr Aziz Abdullah Dwumfuor. Requests are answered case by case
and subject to the same institutional terms.

The third-party papers reviewed during the project are not redistributed here.
Their citations are preserved in the withheld extraction sheet and documentation.

A recorded discrepancy, noted here so it is not lost with the files: the extraction
workbook lists ScienceDirect, IEEE Xplore, and Springer Link in its numeric search
funnel, while the narrative documentation mentions Scopus in its methodology. The
project preserved the original materials rather than silently reconciling this.

The screening totals, model results, and protocol details drawn from these files
remain documented in the repository README, in `../REPRODUCIBILITY.md`, and in the
interactive case study under `../docs/`.
